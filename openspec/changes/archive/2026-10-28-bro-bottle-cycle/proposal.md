## Why

An unconditional pacing loop makes Bro distracting. Fresh profanity should drive a repeatable comic bottle routine, and quieter input should let him sober down.

## What Changes

- Each fresh profanity supplies a bottle. Awake Bro drinks promptly, then tosses the empty bottle aside.
- Rising frequency brings rowdy bottle-led dancing and increasingly careless throws, followed by sleep.
- Bottles arriving during sleep accumulate beside him. On waking he drinks the stash, then either sleeps again or gradually sobers as the input slows.
- Keep the performance inside a stable decorative stage in the app window, independent of analytics filters, sound, burst reminders, and the installed Codex pet.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `mascot-cycle`: Live-driven bottle accounting, escalating character behavior, sleep, and recovery.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Adds a deterministic model in CodexStatsApp and replaces autonomous chart pacing with grounded drinking, throwing, rowdy, and sleeping art. No service, dependency, persisted chat data, or desktop overlay is added.
